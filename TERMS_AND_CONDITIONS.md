- When you join a group, you commit to making all required contributions

### 5.4 Rotation and Payouts

**Rotation Order:**
- Rotation order is determined at group creation (manual or random)
- Once locked, the rotation order cannot be changed
- Each member receives a payout according to their position in the rotation

**Payout Processing:**
- Payouts are initiated by the group administrator after all contributions for the cycle are collected
- Payouts are processed via bank transfer using Paystack
- You must have a verified bank account on file to receive payouts
- Payout processing times depend on Paystack and banking systems (typically 1-2 business days)
- We are not responsible for delays caused by third-party payment processors or banks

**Payout Failures:**
- If a payout fails, funds are returned to the group wallet
- The group administrator may retry the payout
- Failed payouts do not advance the rotation
- You will be notified of payout status via push notification and/or SMS

## 22. Definitions

**Ajo Platform**: The digital thrift-collection platform comprising ajo-mobile, ajo-server, and ajo-admin-web.

**User**: Any individual who accesses or uses our services.

**Group Administrator**: A user who creates a savings group and has administrative privileges within that group.

**Group Member**: A user who has been invited to and accepted membership in a savings group.

**Wallet**: A digital account maintained by Ajo for storing funds used for contributions.

**Contribution**: A payment made by a group member toward the group's collective savings.

**Cycle**: A single rotation period in which one member receives the group's collected funds.

**Payout**: The transfer of funds from the group's central account to the designated recipient for a cycle.

**Default**: A contribution that remains unpaid after its due date has passed.

**Platform Administrator**: An authorized user with administrative access to ajo-admin-web for managing the overall platform.
